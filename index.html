Questions before you hire us

Still unsure? Send your website and we'll answer in your free audit.

Do I need a brand new website?

Not always. The free audit shows whether your current site can be fixed or should be rebuilt. If fixing it is enough, we'll tell you.

How soon will I get more calls?

Tap-to-call, better quote forms and missed-call text-back work from launch day. Google Business Profile improvements usually show within weeks. Higher Google rankings typically take three to six months, and anyone promising faster is guessing.

Do you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We guarantee the work in your written scope, honest monthly reports, and straight advice about what's working.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, content and site belong to you. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

How much of my time will this take?

About an hour at the start to share your services, the towns you cover, photos of your work and access to your Google Business Profile. We handle the rest.

You're based in the Philippines. How does communication work?

You talk directly with Aldrin by email or WhatsApp, with replies within one business day and working hours that overlap with US mornings and evenings.
